> What size tiles are you making. I'm working on a similar game but I think my tiles are too big. They're 64x64 and I think it'll make some problems down the road.

I’m working with 16x16 tiles–they’re small, but I’d struggle if I had to fill bigger tiles with enough detail. I think 32x32 tends to be the largest size that people do on average. I don’t think 64x64 is impossible, but if you’re struggling, try going smaller!
