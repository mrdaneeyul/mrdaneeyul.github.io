Nobody on the island–not even the adventurous Pono–has met the mysterious old lady in the woods. Some say if you stand up on the cliffs by the castle on a moonless night, you can look out over the black waters to the tiny sister island and see strange lights waxing and waning. Magic from another age, maybe will-o’-the-wisps. Or *maybe* the myths about the Dragon Kings are true and someone does live out there–watching and waiting for some unknown purpose.

I actually had some trouble with this one, because I’d already drawn another old woman (whom you may have seen in the rolling gif and dialogue tests!). It meant I had to figure out how to differentiate them in a visible way other than just the obvious color change–I didn’t want just a palette swap. Lots of experimentation and redrawing finally led to a unique hairstyle, and everything branched out from there!

She’s actually one of the shortest NPCs I have (if not the shortest). She was originally made shorter in order to fit a hairbun on top, but even after I changed the hairstyle, I left her short. I like it because it makes her look hunched. Perfect for the character. :)
