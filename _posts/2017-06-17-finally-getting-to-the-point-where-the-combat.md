Finally getting to the point where the combat animation feels GOOD.

I had grand plans to completely implement the new combat system, record Tav fighting a ferocious monster, and slap it on the internet to reap the rewards of fame and glory. As usual, this did not come to pass. :)

The animation system is working well already in the game, and I’m working on drawing up the animations themselves. Hopefully we’ll have awesome combat in a week or two!

Regardless, I’m pleased with how this came out. Thanks to the [#constructive-criticism channel on the Pixel Art discord](https://discord.gg/AXYwv9). Got some *fantastic* tips for improvement. There still may be some more work to be done, but we’re getting there!
