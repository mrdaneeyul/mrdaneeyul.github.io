We held a very important [poll on Twitter](https://twitter.com/MrDaneeyul/status/858835734710628354) and your voices have been heard! Next up is **Dak the Lumberjack**!

Dak lives in the woods and don’t care about no monsters! People need wood! Somebody’s gotta chop down those trees! A true man’s man, Dak only speaks in grunts, and he keeps his beard (but not his hairdo) finely groomed. His apparent bulk is actually a barrel chest of pure muscle. Many a village maiden has swooned at his passing.

The original was one of the more difficult sprites we worked on–trying to fit all that plaid detail into one sprite *without* it looking blobby was harder than expected. Along with Zhar’s Daughter, his original sprite came in first place for most colors used: **6**! That’s with a limit of **3** colors. I think he might be the only character that I’ve wished I could break the color limit… just to give him some fancy blue jeans! But I couldn’t live with myself if I did that.
