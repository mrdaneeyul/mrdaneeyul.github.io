It was an excellent vacation, the longest I’ve had time off since I graduated from college. **Three weeks. :D** I spent probably a third of the time getting a crazy amount of stuff done on The Waking Cloak, another third messing around with random fun projects just because, and the rest playing video games (highlights being Bloodborne and Child of Light!) and reading.<br>

I’m actually pretty okay with going back to work–it’s good structure to my day, and lunch is a great time to work on the game. I won’t be blazing fast like I was over the break, but it’ll be easier to be consistent.

Enemy AI is still on the agenda. I’ve got my test enemy confined and moving around better, so I’m gonna keep working on that!
