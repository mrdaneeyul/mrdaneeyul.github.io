I’m sad to report that this month’s NaNoWriMo didn’t get off the ground! It’s the first time in five years that I haven’t reached 50,000 words. In fact, I only reached about **1,600 words.**

I said I’d go easy this year, and I did, but I can’t help but feel a bit disappointed I didn’t get any further. The idea was to do vignettes and short stories for The Waking Cloak, and while I got some decent outlining and lore done (which was actually really helpful), ultimately my motivation wasn’t quite enough.<br>

I do feel a little like I let you guys down after that sneaky trailer from several weeks ago. The good news is I did write up “what happened” as far as the stories and such go, it’s just not in a very readable or interesting format. I hope you’ll forgive me for that!

## There is good news though!

I’ve had the motivation to work on a side project of mine that’s been stewing a while called **Genogatchi**. This is a mashup of Monster Rancher, the Chao Garden from SA2B, and *randomly generated creatures*. The scope is nice and small on it, and I’m hoping to get it finished up by the end of January 2019.

Here’s a (very WIP) example! I’ve got debug options turned on so that a new monster gets generated each time you click. Here you can see some of the variety. :)
