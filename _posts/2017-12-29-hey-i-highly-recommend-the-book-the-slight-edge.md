> Hey! I highly recommend the book "The Slight Edge" by Jeff Olson. Do you have any favorites of your own?

*[For context, this was asked after the [motivation post](http://www.thewakingcloak.com/post/168645317874/your-project-is-very-inspiring-can-i-ask-how-do)]*

Awesome, I’ll try to check that out sometime!

I don’t read a lot of nonfiction, so I’m having a bit of trouble thinking up some favorites! Like I mentioned in the post, I like [The War of Art, by Steven Pressfield](https://smile.amazon.com/War-Art-Steven-Pressfield-ebook/dp/B007A4SDCG). Another one that really affected me was [A Million Miles in a Thousand Years, by Donald Miller](https://smile.amazon.com/Million-Miles-Thousand-Years-Learned/dp/1400202981). [Bird By Bird, by Anne Lamott](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/12543.Bird_by_Bird) is an excellent one.
