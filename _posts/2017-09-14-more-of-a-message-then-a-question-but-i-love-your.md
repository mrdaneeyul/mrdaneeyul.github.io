Thanks for the kind words. :) And I’m so glad it helped!

I try to never use straight white, at least in my pixel art–I think it stands out too much. The very, very light yellow I use for most everything has the added bonus of making it look retro; “white” on a Game Boy Color screen was more like an super light olive/brown-ish color (essentially the color of the screen itself, since the system isn’t backlit).
