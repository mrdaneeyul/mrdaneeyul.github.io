Here’s my new, revolutionary block-pushing mechanic… and a rare glimpse one of my prototype rooms for [#**TheWakingCloak**](https://twitter.com/hashtag/TheWakingCloak?src=hash)! The neon colors motivate me!

The prototype room lets me explore and refine mechanics before I put them in the wider world of the main game, which has a lot more code. It’s a good way to untangle and refactor without getting lost. I originally was attempting to make it as ugly as possible by picking horrible colors, but it actually ended up looking kinda neat…<br>
