**Bipso** is that kid.

You know, the one that–despite never being fed blazberries or anything even remotely containing sugar–still has more energy than everyone in the entire village. Combined. His parents have received plenty of unsolicited advice from concerned neighbors, but none of it has worked. Their only hope is that he goes into the same field as Seni, his older sister, which will allow him to run everywhere for the rest of his days, but he’s been quoted saying, “That sounds boring. When is dinner ready? Ooh, is that a bug?”

Also, when interviewed about his talents, he reportedly said, “Talking and running,” except with a lot more words and a lot more movement.

Bipso was a really early sprite. I saved a sprite of “[Bipsom](https://zelda.gamepedia.com/Bipin_and_Blossom%27s_Son)” (aka the non-canonical name for Bipin and Blossom’s son from *Oracle of Ages*) and told my brother I wanted a tiny, hyper kid like that. Of course, he readily agreed. I don’t even remember if the original sprite was actually supposed to be the tiny, hyper kid, but that’s what I transformed him into! :D

Beyond that, I pretty much just decided to make him smaller. And fixed his… uh… feet. And gave him funky hair. And fixed his palette.
