Hey, everyone! I’m back! :D

For the month of November, I’ve been writing a draft of the story for The Waking Cloak. 50,000 words later, I’ve completed this year’s NaNoWriMo. :)<br>

It’s been a great journey. I’ve learned so much about this world and its characters, and I can’t wait to share it with you all.

-   More NPC art<br>

-   Basic enemy AI<br>
