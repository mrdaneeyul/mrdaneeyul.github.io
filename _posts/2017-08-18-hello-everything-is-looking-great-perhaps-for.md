> Hello, everything is looking great! Perhaps for the dodge roll you can only use it 3 times in a row, or the hero becomes dizzy for a little while. It'd make players take a break from it, but not too much.

Hi there! And thank you. :)

Thanks for the suggestion as well! I’m grateful people are taking the time to give me such good input. Dizziness is a feature I’d like to consider adding if rolling gets too out of control or makes things way too easy.

Part of my problem was overthinking it, I think. Somehow, I never considered a simple roll with no invincibility frames! So the plan now is to just have it be a burst of speed, but at a loss of precise control. And maybe if you roll into something, you bonk your head and have to wait a bit while you recover from your concussion.

So I’m going to go with the simplest solution first and see how it plays. Maybe get a few people to test it too! Dizziness is a good fallback for rolling too much in too little time.

Thanks again!!
