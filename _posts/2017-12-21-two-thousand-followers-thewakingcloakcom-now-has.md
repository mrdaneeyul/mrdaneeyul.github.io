TheWakingCloak.com now has **2,000 followers** (AND as of this posting, there’s also nearly **900 followers** on my [Twitter account, @MrDaneeyul](http://www.twitter.com/mrdaneeyul)!)  I’m so amazed and incredibly thankful for all of you (yes, even you bots!).  At the beginning of 2017, there were only a few hundred followers. I never dreamed The Waking Cloak would have almost 3,000 total. And we’re just getting started.

It’s mind-boggling and humbling that so many people would even have a passing interest in my little game. I will continue to pour my heart into The Waking Cloak in your honor. I hope that you’ll continue to follow along, and I hope that, when the game finally does come out, you’ll have a labor of love on your hands that you really enjoy playing. :)

**Thank you all**
