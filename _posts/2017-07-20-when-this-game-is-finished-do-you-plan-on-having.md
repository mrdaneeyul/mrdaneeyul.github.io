> when this game is finished, do you plan on having it on steam, its own website, or something else?

Yep! The initial plan is to put it on Steam for PC (and Mac and Linux if I can get the builds running). I’m also going to put it on itch.io, since I get more of the profit from the game, and itch.io is very friendly to indie devs. I’m going to submit to GoG if possible, but they’re known for being more stringent with who they allow on their marketplace (which is a good thing, imo!).

I’d like to port to consoles, but that’s pretty expensive. Depends on how the game does initially! This would be for Xbox One and PlayStation 4–GameMaker doesn’t have any plans to port to Switch at the moment, sadly!

But anyway, yes–Steam and itch.io for PC are a given, and I’ll do my best on the rest!
