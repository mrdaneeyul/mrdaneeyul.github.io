Also a bit more AI research.<br><br>**Thursday**<br>Did some learnin’ on music composition!<br><br>**Friday**<br>Wrote a bit of AI pseudocode before I got hit with some inspiration for a boss concept. I drew that the only way I know how–with pixels!<br><br>**Saturday**<br>Did some more AI work. Trying to make it more like it should have been originally–the AI has its own inputs almost like a digital controller. They can push left, right, up, down, attack, etc. The AI itself is just a method of choosing which “button” to press. Also fixed a super annoying bug where you could only double attack. Turns out every single actor in the game that didn’t have an AI was queuing up an attack for the player when the button was pressed. Everything in the queue was getting cleared out except the first two attacks. Oops. All better now!<br>
