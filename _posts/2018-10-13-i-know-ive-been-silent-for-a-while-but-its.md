I know I’ve been silent for a while, but it’s because I’ve been **very hard at work** on this for you guys. :)

## The New Inventory Screen

This ended up being a massive undertaking. I thought I’d just clean up the old inventory screen a bit, make it look a touch nicer, and get rid of a few of the things that were obsolete. Little did I know that I was going to spend about a month hammering away at every aspect of the game in order to complete the redesign.

Here’s the ***old*** version!

![image](/Images/devlog/2018-10-13-i-know-ive-been-silent-for-a-while-but-its-2.png)

And the new one, for easier comparison:

![image](/Images/devlog/2018-10-13-i-know-ive-been-silent-for-a-while-but-its-3.png)

So what all changed?

-   I redrew **every single sprite AAAAAAAAH** (seriously, this took forever)<br>
-   Added a map (which necessitated having the overworld more or less laid out)–it’s not functional yet, but someday…<br>
-   Switched the HUD to be darker and thus easier on the eyes<br>
-   Added amulet and tablet fragments to the HUD beside gems
-   Added a mini-map to the HUD
-   Got rid of the mana (I’d dropped it a long time ago but it just kind of sat there on the inventory screen anyway)
-   Simplified the frame (for a while, the frame corners even had white “bracket” things making them *too* fancy and distracting!)
-   Rearranged pretty much everything
-   Dropped useless key items, including the seed box, the egg, the water bracelet, the scroll, the thorn, two keys, and the four orbs
-   Finalized dungeon concepts/location concepts/item sequence (this *alone* took a week or two) so that I could finalize my equipment
-   Cut and trimmed equipment: dropped the mirror, simplified the staff, re-added the wind item as a fan (used to be Soli’s Satchel back when we first created the game!), and upgraded the ring from a key item (partly taking over one of the staff functionalities)
-   Added a pretty background that will reflect whether it’s day or night~
-   Added the orbs frame… these will mark your progress through the game
-   Lots and lots of tweaking everything, etc., etc., ad infinitum

I’m **extremely pleased** with the final result (though “final” is a loose term–it’ll change as the game continues to evolve, just not as drastically), and I hope you are too. This was a great exercise that really solidified a lot of important stuff. I hope you enjoy! :)
