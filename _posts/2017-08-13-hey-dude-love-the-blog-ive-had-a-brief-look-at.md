HEY, thanks for stopping and looking around. Glad you like it. :)

GameMaker has its own scripting language called GML (GameMaker Language, appropriately, haha). It’s a little like other scripting languages, such as JavaScript or Lua. It’s very easy to get into, especially if you’ve done any programming before. While it has its quirks, it gets the job done.

Beginning by experimentation is perfect. I’d suggest pulling up some tutorials and walking through them step by step. Also I have a [post about getting started](http://www.thewakingcloak.com/post/163536219054/hey-ive-been-following-this-blog-for-a-while)that you should read! :)

Enjoy. Making games is the best hobby I’ve ever taken part in!
