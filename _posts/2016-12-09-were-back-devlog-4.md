“We” in the royal sense, since it’s just me :)

 

What have I been up to?

 

NaNoWriMo went well, and I wrote over 50,000 words of a comedy/satire novel called *The Inter-Dimensional Protagonist Rescue Agency*. The middle was a real struggle, since I got caught in a rut of debilitating perfectionism. I kept thinking, “This won’t be good enough. I’m not good enough to be a writer,” and slowed way, way down. But near the end, I decided I’d write scenes in any order I pleased, and things got much more fun from there.

 

I also spent a lot of time working on a website contract for a client. It’s not complete yet, but we’re in the home stretch.

 

But now…

 

I’m working on The Waking Cloak again!!

 

I couldn’t be more pumped. It’s been fantastic to catch up again, to sit down and work on this passion project. I’ve been hard at work drawing with pixels and figuring out a plan for what to do next. We’ve got a mockup for #screenshotsaturday tomorrow (which you’ve already seen if you’re signed up for [the newsletter](http://ink.us13.list-manage1.com/subscribe?u=f1ee90c5489698511c979fa0b&id=0ddf2e8e4c)), and hopefully I’ll get to do some dev streaming soon.

 

Also as mentioned in the newsletter:

 

-   Investigating GameMaker Studio 2. It seems to have some *really* helpful features for a game like mine. The import feature works well for porting from GameMaker Studio 1, especially for a game so early in development like this one, so moving over is a major possibility.
-   Will be working on fixing a bug that flings you across the room when you attempt to go to the next screen. Can’t have that…
-   Will also be working on smoothing out diagonal movement.
-   Looking into a chiptune tracker program called [DefleMask](http://www.deflemask.com/). I’ve been using the wonderful [FamiTracke](http://famitracker.com/)r, but DefleMask has a GameBoy setting, which is closer to the authenticity I’m looking for than FamiTracker’s NES abilities.<br>

And that’s it! We’ll get back into the swing of things again soon, with more technical posts, more screenshots/mockups, and more design discussions. See you next time!
