My only regret is not setting up autotile earlier I was a fool, this is SO MUCH EASIER AHHHH

Here’s how it works. Aseprite has the ability to convert layers to tilemap (in the beta only, currently). It find all the tiles that are the same and puts them into a tile “palette”. If you update any of the tiles anywhere on the image, it will update all the other tiles in the image.

[@JoelLikesPigs on Twitter showed me his awesome Aseprite template](https://twitter.com/JoelLikesPigs/status/1404391868415320064) a while back and it’s what I’ve been using here!

![](/Images/devlog/2022-05-14-my-only-regret-is-not-setting-up-autotile-earlier-2.png)

The template is neat because you can copy the top left section and put it straight into your tile sprite
