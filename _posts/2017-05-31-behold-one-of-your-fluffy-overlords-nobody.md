BEHOLD, ONE OF YOUR FLUFFY OVERLORDS.

Nobody suspects a thing. How could they, with such an adorable happy face? But it’s rumored that there’s more than meets the eye with the cats. Some kind of secret society… a hidden agency where they are the bearers of the deepest knowledge of the island… but that’s just a silly rumor.

Isaac was just drawing a bunch of these for fun (because, c’mon, cats). I’m pretty sure we gave up on the original cat halfway through since getting its legs to work right was proving… difficult, lol. This was an interesting remake because I kept drawing him way too big, and it was hard to fit details into the smaller versions!
