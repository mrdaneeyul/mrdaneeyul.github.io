> Also what do you use to make your art? do you practically draw it pixel by pixel?

Pixel art is a fun little beast. You can technically use Windows Paint to do it, though you’d be missing some amenities.

I personally use [**Aseprite**](https://www.aseprite.org/). It’s the first one I tried, and I’ve been very happy with it. I’ve heard fantastic things about [**Pyxel Edit**](http://pyxeledit.com/), and **[GraphicsGale](https://graphicsgale.com/us/)** is another good one that’s now free!

And yes–I draw my art pixel by pixel for all intents and purposes. :) Of course, there ARE line tools, paint fill tools, etc., so I didn’t precisely place each pixel. That would be exhausting, haha. I also stick to a list of rules I have to mimic what the GameBoy Color could render (3 colors for sprites, 4 colors for tiles, etc.). While you don’t have to stick to those rules, it’s a good principle to keep a small number of colors on your palette.

If you want to get started,[reddit.com/r/pixelart](https://www.reddit.com/r/PixelArt/) is a good place to post for constructive criticism. Also, that subreddit links to some fantastic resources–[how to get started](http://pixeljoint.com/forum/forum_posts.asp?TID=11299) and a [big list of tutorials](https://www.reddit.com/r/PixelArt/comments/gxmgh/pixel_art_tutorials/).

Enjoy. :)
