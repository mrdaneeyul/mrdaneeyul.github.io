The ProtoDungeon makes a reappearance!! :D

This is where I’ve been testing a lot of my code. It helps to work in a place that almost entirely ignores aesthetics so that I can get the mechanics and feel down. If it feels good while it looks like this, surely it’ll be even better once I add the art and effects. :)

In this case, I’m getting my old block pushing code to work with the collision code I updated recently. In the process, I found out I could make pushing the jars feel even better than pushing the blocks, so that’s nice!

Finally, a bit of a mini-announcement:

## The ProtoDungeon is going to be the very first “demo” of The Waking Cloak!

It’ll show off the swap mechanics while not spoiling anything from the game–it’ll be a completely different scenario. This will also get me some good practice on dungeon building and, of course, feedback!

I don’t know at this point when it will be available (will probably be several weeks at least), but I believe I will first make it available to [Patreon patrons](https://www.patreon.com/mrdaneeyul), and then to those on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/v7rf8h2).
