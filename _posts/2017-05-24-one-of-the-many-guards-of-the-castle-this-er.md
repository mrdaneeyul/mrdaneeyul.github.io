One of the many **guards of the castle**, this… er… totally non-generic guard absolutely does not look the same as every other guard. He has his own backstory and… uh… probably his own family. *Shuffles through papers* Which one was this again? I swear, I’m not having trouble telling them apart. It’s got to be the armor.

My brother did a great job on this sprite already, so there wasn’t much to change. The biggest struggle was giving it some movement (especially the feet… I don’t know why I struggled so much with the feet… and now all I can do is stare at the feet). Poor guy had dinky armaments before, so I made them a little more menacing, and then took a page from the Oracle of Ages handbook and bobbed them up and down.
