Welcome to the brand-spanking-new devblog for The Waking Cloak!

You may be asking yourself, "How did I get here? What is my purpose in life? What does this even mean?” Or perhaps you’re not asking yourself these things at all.

Either way, I can only really answer the last question: The Waking Cloak is a Game Boy Color style video game I’m developing. It’s inspired by all kinds of games from my childhood. More on that later! If you’d like to know more about the game, please check out the [FAQ page](http://www.thewakingcloak.com/faq).

My plan is to post once a week, but no less than once per month. I’ll talk about design plans, give visual and audio updates, ramble about game mechanics, and so forth. I don’t know what’s interesting to you guys, but I’m basically just going to be passionate, geek out, and maybe get super technical sometimes, and if that’s too much silliness for you, well, I still kinda hope you stay.

So please feel free to follow along. Throw comments or messages at me. Enjoy yourself. Dance around. You get the idea–it’s good to have you here, and I hope to make you feel involved in this crazy fun process of building a game.
