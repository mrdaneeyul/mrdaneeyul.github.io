Ladies and gentlemen…

Puppies and kittens and the tiniest of birds…

## The Waking Cloak is on Patreon!

If you like, you can help me bring my dream to life for as little as $1 per month! Every bit matters.

Just click on the image above ~*[or right here](http://www.patreon.com/mrdaneeyul)~* to head on over!

If you’d like to help support but can’t do so financially, you still count! **Reblogs** are free and help more than you know. :)

So what will I be using this funding for?

In short: *making The Waking Cloak!*

In long:

-   Various game development tools
-   Potential artists for cover art and promotional stuffz!
-   Attending conferences and events!
-   Rental of a PO Box to allow me to keep my privacy for the business and mailing list
-   [TheWakingCloak.com](http://thewakingcloak.com/) domain name and eventual website hosting (tumblr is free, but I’m gonna make a dedicated site at some point)
-   Legal and business fees (such as trademark registration and so on)
-   Translations/localizations??
-   Marketing - getting the word out!

A quick note: I will not be cutting any past or future content from here to post on Patreon. A lot of Patreons have patron-exclusive content (and there’s nothing wrong with that!), but a video game doesn’t lend itself super well to exclusive content.

What patrons *do* get is earlier access to that same stuff, along with a few other goodies as a thank you for super-believing in The Waking Cloak!

![](/Images/devlog/2017-07-06-ladies-and-gentlemen-puppies-and-kittens-and-the-2.gif)

**So please–reblog and/or head over to the Patreon to lend your support!** Thank you for your time, and I hope you have a wonderful day. :)
