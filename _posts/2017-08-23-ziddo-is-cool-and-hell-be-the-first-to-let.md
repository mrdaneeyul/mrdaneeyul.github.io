**Ziddo** is cool, and he’ll be the first to let everyone know just how much cooler he is than you. He’s got it all: looks, charm, muscles, a smooth hairdo, and a sweet v-neck. When Ziddo enters a room, everyone’s eyes are drawn to him. He’s always bragging about his success with the ladies.

So then… why leave his home and jump on a refugee ship to cross the sea for the Eastern Continent when you’ve got it this good? Well, the refugees could use some eye candy, and so the entire world has a chance to bask in your glory, of course! Big fish needs a big pond!

Another early remake I did! First thing (after correcting his color palette) was to give him some more movement. Just moving the head wasn’t enough, of course, so I messed with his arms–for once, the arms didn’t give me trouble!

What did give me trouble this time was the clothes. Nothing I tried looked right… until I accidentally gave him a really wide neckline. I couldn’t stop giggling at how silly it looked, and so I pulled it down even farther until he had a deep v-neck. Inspired and amused by this stroke of luck, I finished off the sprite by decorating his belt so it didn’t look like he had a midriff.

At the beginning, I knew I wanted an egotistical bully-ish character that claimed credit for most of the things you did as a player. Once Ziddo’s sprite was done, I knew I’d found him. :D
