It’s been a long time since I’ve had a mockup ready for you guys, since mainly I’ve been working on coding. **But!** Over the past few weeks I’ve been forming the tileset for towns. I am *extremely* proud of the mockup that came out of it, and it’ll be up on **Saturday**. YEAHHHH

So what *have* I been working on?

-   Inventory system<br>
-   Dialogue system<br>
-   Options system<br>
-   Revisiting subpixels (yes, AGAIN. will probably spawn a “Adventures in SubPixel Space, Part 3″ post.)<br>
-   NPCs (art and code)<br>
-   Tile collision system (the old one was crappy and broke down when I reintroduced subpixels; the new one is *much cleaner* and actually works. We’re talking two scripts instead of ten).

What will I be working on next?

-   Fixing game-breaking bugs (combat currently crashes the game, yay!)<br>
-   Continuing to introduce NPCs into the game<br>
-   Expanding room sizes. This will not only allow for the camera to stretch its limbs and the game to feel a bit more open/breathable, but also allow support for different monitor resolutions.
-   Camera tweaks for panning around bigger rooms
