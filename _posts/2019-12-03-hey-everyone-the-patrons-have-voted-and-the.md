Hey everyone!

The patrons have voted, and the results are in: ProtoDungeon: Episode III’s theme will be the lotus boots! Current (subject to change) plans for this episode:

**Lotus Boots**

-   Location: Shipwreck Cove
-   New mechanics: jumping, swimming, dashing, rolling
-   Will include two enemy types and one boss

I hope you’ll all look forward to this! I am intending to start work in January–less than a month away! In the meantime, I will be working on cleaning up Episodes I and II and hopefully releasing the Mac version of both of these.

Here are some further stats from the patron vote! Everyone ranked each item in the order they’d like to see it. The lotus boots were the clear winner here, although the sword/chalice might have taken first place if a group of voters hadn’t put it last on their list. ;)

Anyway, this has been fun, and I look forward to wrapping up Episode III so we can do the Episode IV poll, haha!
