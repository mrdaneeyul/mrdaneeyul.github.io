The final (~for now~) version of the east-facing sword attack! I redid a **bunch** of details after 1) great feedback on the size of the slash and sword positioning, and 2) discovering the sword “smear” looked super weird when rotated 90 degrees for the south-facing attack.

My original thought was to make the sword smear and the sword into a singular hitbox. It would be the exact same animation in all four directions. This is what the old Zelda games did. However, the only thing I could get looking “right” at the time was about a 45-degree angle slash, which isn’t very powerful. Take a look at this old gif (the red is a hitbox test, not a lightsaber… although now that I mention it…)

![](/Images/devlog/2017-06-20-the-final-for-now-version-of-the-east-facing-2.gif)

What helped with the new animation is separating the sword smear from the actual blade itself. I decided that only the smear would be the hitbox, and the sword could just go wherever it looked best. (This idea actually came from Crawl–the game is a bit gory for me, but the artist has some great [**videos**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ys-tiUXIxjQ)and [**articles**](http://www.gamasutra.com/view/news/300004/Game_Design_Deep_Dive_Creating_the_striking_pixel_art_of_Crawl.php?utm_source=dlvr.it&utm_medium=twitter)on animation.)

It was a freeing decision, but still a bunch of work! Mainly because I’m still developing my animation skills. The current method is just to attempt *every single combination of pixels that could possibly exist* by brute force until something looks right. :) Needless to say, this one’s definitely been a lot of hard work and frustration. While there may still be stuff to do, I couldn’t be more pleased with how it’s turning out.

And it’s a big step in the redesign of the combat system–making it feel fluid and alive is a top priority!
