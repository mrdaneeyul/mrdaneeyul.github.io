> Hello Daniel! I have been following progress on The Waking Cloak everyday since i found it, as I am a big fan of Zelda, and I fell in love with your game's Oracle/Link's Awakening art style. I think the game so far looks super promising, and your streams inspired me to purchase Gamemaker studio 2! I have 2 questions: 1. When will you do a new development stream? 2. Since you are gathering a small fan base, are you open to the idea of making a Discord server to make communication easy? Cheers :)

Hey! Thanks so much for following along. :) It means a lot to me. And I’m so pleased you were inspired to pick up GameMaker–I hope you enjoy it!

**Answer 1 - Gamedev streaming)** Still TBD! Previously, it was based off when my wife was at work, since I don’t like to inconvenience her by streaming while she’s home. However, I’m considering doing lunchtime streams 2x a week (when I work from home). That may start up soon, but it depends on some scheduling stuff in the coming weeks.I will announce here and on [Twitter](http://www.twitter.com/mrdaneeyul)when we get started again! :)<br>

**Answer 2 - Discord server)** Here’s a secret–I’ve already started the process of creating a Discord server! :D However, I want to be smart about this. I’d hate to start up a server and have it stall out from lack of momentum/users. I’ve seen similar situations where a dev will set up a subreddit which only ends up with a handful of active subscribers–it can deflate some of the interest in the game, especially for newcomers. With that in mind, I have three milestones that I want to accomplish before I launch the server:

1.  2-3 equipment mechanics ready (probably sword and one of the staff spells). This is so that I have some meatier stuff that I (and you guys!) can pass around the internet.
2.  A thread up on [TIGSource](https://forums.tigsource.com/index.php?board=27.0) and on the [GameMaker Forums](https://forum.yoyogames.com/index.php?forums/work-in-progress.18/). This is pretty reliant on #1, but should be easy enough to complete.
3.  A certain number of devlog followers so that the starting audience for the server is higher. Follower growth here has been quite strong, so I’m optimistic!<br>

In short–I’ll probably launch the Discord server sometime in the next couple months. Once again, keep an eye on here and/or Twitter, because I’ll make sure to announce it in both places.

Thanks for your great questions! :)
